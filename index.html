One locker point, built for daily life

Short, wide boxes stack in rows and columns, so a locker bank fits small spaces and grows with demand.

Your own locker

Rent a personal box locker through the app, by the day or for longer, with the number always shown on your phone.

Delivery handoff

Create a one-time code, share it with the driver, and the order lands in your locker while you stay in class.

Simple, secure access

Open with a personal PIN. Every locker carries a large number that matches the app.

Charging built in

Charge phones and laptops inside the locker. A timer switches the power off automatically.

Camera and tamper alerts

A small internal camera records on events, and forced opening alerts the owner, campus security and the admin.

Built to keep working

A backup battery and offline PIN access keep lockers usable through power or Wi-Fi interruptions.

Deliveries without the wait

Instead of meeting the driver, you share a single-use code. It opens only your drop-off locker, once, and never reveals your own PIN.

  1. OrderChoose the campus locker as the delivery point.
  2. GenerateCreate a one-time code in the app.
  3. ShareSend it to the driver with the order.
  4. Drop-offThe driver enters the code and loads the locker.
  5. NotifyThe locker locks and you get an alert.

One platform, many places

PodIt is built as a platform. Each site gets its own locker map, rules and admin dashboard inside the same app.

Universities

Student lockers, delivery handoff and charging, set up around each campus’s own rules.

Offices

Personal storage, parcel reception without a front desk, and secure charging for staff.

Gyms and clubs

Safe storage for bags and kit, with quick and secure access.

Residential buildings

Delivery lockers so residents can receive parcels when they are out.
